Camera Check

Checks what this phone's cameras and browser can do. It takes about half a minute. Nothing leaves your phone on its own: when it's done, you send the result as a small file to whoever shared this page with you.

1Check this phone

Takes up to half a minute. Your browser will ask to use the camera: tap Allow. Each camera turns on for a moment; you don't need to aim it at anything.

    Cameras

    This browser doesn't share its cameras' focus or zoom details. If Chrome is on this phone, running the check in Chrome as well gives the full picture. The close-up test below still works here.

    2Close-up test optional

    Grab a card from your wallet with some small print on it: a gift card, loyalty card or business card is ideal. Each back camera gets 5 seconds: hold the card upright and move it until it fills the dashed box from top to bottom (closer for wide lenses), keep it flat and still in good light, and the page measures how sharp it looks. The picture stays on your screen; nothing is saved or sent.

    3Send the result

    Tap Send as file and pick Discord (or Messages, email…), then the chat with the person who shared this page. On a computer, Save file and drag it into the chat.
